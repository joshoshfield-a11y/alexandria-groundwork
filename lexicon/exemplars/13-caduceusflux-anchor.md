## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** two coupled subsystems A and B with a conserved
   total quantity E = E_A + E_B (phase space: positions and momenta
   of both, or energy shares (e, E−e)).
2. **Computable form:** coupled-oscillator exchange,
   ẍ₁ + ω²x₁ = −κ(x₁−x₂), ẍ₂ + ω²x₂ = −κ(x₂−x₁). Energy sloshes
   between the two at the beat frequency Δω ≈ κ/ω. Worked example:
   two identical pendulums (ω = 2π rad/s) weakly coupled; starting
   with only pendulum 1 displaced, pendulum 2's amplitude grows to
   full and back with period 2π/Δω — complete periodic transfer.
3. **Measurable quantity:** energy transferred, in joules;
   exchange rate in watts. Instrument: displacement sensors plus
   energy bookkeeping, or calorimetry on each subsystem.
4. **Standard theory:** classical mechanics of coupled systems;
   conservation of total energy (Noether: time-translation
   symmetry); normal-mode decomposition. The quantum analogue is
   the exchange interaction J·S₁·S₂ in Heisenberg magnets — the
   same "dynamic exchange" with spin operators instead of
   pendulums.
5. **Delta assessment:** none. This operator is conserved-quantity
   exchange between coupled subsystems — "dynamic exchange" is the
   standard coupled-oscillator / kinetic-exchange picture.
