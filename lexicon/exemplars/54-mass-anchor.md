## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** non-negative scalar m ≥ 0 attached to a body — the inertial parameter in its equation of motion.
2. **Computable form:** inertial mass m = F/a; gravitational mass from W = mg. Worked example: a cart accelerates at a = 2.5 m/s² under a measured push F = 10 N → m = F/a = 4 kg. Equivalently, weighing gives W = 39.2 N at g = 9.81 m/s² → m = 4 kg, testing the equivalence principle to the instrument's precision.
3. **Measurable quantity:** kilograms (kg). Instrument: analytical balance against calibrated standards; since the 2019 SI revision the kilogram is defined via the fixed Planck constant h, realized by Kibble balance.
4. **Standard theory:** Newtonian mechanics (inertia, F = ma); general relativity (equivalence of inertial and gravitational mass); the SI Brochure (9th ed.) for the unit definition.
5. **Delta assessment:** none. This operator is the standard mass concept under a mythic name.
