## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** smooth scalar potential functions V: ℝ³ → ℝ; a well is a local minimum basin of V.
2. **Computable form:** F = −∇V; bound states exist where total energy E < V at the basin rim. Worked example: harmonic well V(x) = ½kx² with k = 200 N/m. At displacement x = 0.05 m: F = −kx = −10 N (restoring), stored energy U = ½kx² = 0.25 J. Escape requires energy exceeding the rim barrier ΔV.
3. **Measurable quantity:** force in newtons (N) via spring gauge or load cell; potential energy in joules (J) via calorimetry or work integration. The well depth ΔV in joules sets the escape threshold.
4. **Standard theory:** classical mechanics — conservative forces, Hooke's law, the virial relation for bound systems; quantum wells (finite square well) are the same concept quantized.
5. **Delta assessment:** none. This operator is the standard force-from-potential relation under a mythic name.
