## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** a monochromatic plane wave propagating through space — a light wave, a sound wave, a matter wave.
2. **Computable form:** ψ(r, t) = A·exp(i(k·r − ωt)), where the wave vector k points along the propagation direction with magnitude |k| = 2π/λ (angular form; the spectroscopic form 1/λ is its scaled sibling). Momentum connection: p = ℏk for a quantum particle. Worked example: green light at λ = 500 nm has |k| = 2π/(500×10⁻⁹) ≈ 1.257×10⁷ m⁻¹, pointing along the beam; doubling the frequency doubles |k|. In a crystal, k lives in reciprocal space and diffraction occurs when the scattering vector equals a reciprocal-lattice vector (Laue condition).
3. **Measurable quantity:** magnitude in m⁻¹ (radians per meter); direction as a unit vector or angles. Instrument: diffraction grating or spectrometer for |k| via λ; goniometer / detector geometry for direction.
4. **Standard theory:** classical wave kinematics; reciprocal-space crystallography (Laue/Bragg); the de Broglie relation in quantum mechanics.
5. **Delta assessment:** none. This operator is the standard wave vector under a mythic name.
