## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** a damped oscillator (mass m, natural frequency
   ω₀, damping γ) driven by F(t) = F₀cos(ωt).
2. **Computable form:** steady-state amplitude
   A(ω) = (F₀/m) / √((ω₀²−ω²)² + (γω)²), peaking at
   ω ≈ ω₀ with height F₀/(mγω₀) (the Q factor is ω₀/γ).
   Worked example: m = 1 kg, ω₀ = 10 rad/s, γ = 0.5 s⁻¹,
   F₀ = 1 N → resonant amplitude A ≈ 1/(1·0.5·10) = 0.2 m,
   Q = 20; off-resonance at ω = 5, A ≈ 0.013 m. The electrical
   twin is the series RLC circuit, resonating at ω₀ = 1/√(LC)
   with the identical amplitude formula in volts and amps —
   one mathematics, mechanical and electrical incarnations.
3. **Measurable quantity:** resonant frequency in hertz;
   amplitude in meters (or volts, for circuits). Instrument:
   spectrum analyzer / network analyzer sweeping the drive
   frequency and recording the response peak.
4. **Standard theory:** the driven damped harmonic oscillator;
   resonance as matched drive frequency; Q factor; applications
   from tuned circuits to Mössbauer spectroscopy.
5. **Delta assessment:** none. This operator is resonance — the
   amplified response when drive matches natural frequency —
   under a mythic name.
