## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** thermodynamic state (T, P, V) of a substance
   near a phase boundary; order parameter ψ (e.g. magnetization,
   gap) that vanishes above T_c.
2. **Computable form:** from the van der Waals equation,
   (∂P/∂V)_T = (∂²P/∂V²)_T = 0 gives T_c = 8a/(27Rb).
   Worked example: water, a = 0.5537 Pa·m⁶/mol²,
   b = 3.05×10⁻⁵ m³/mol, R = 8.314 J/(mol·K) →
   T_c ≈ 647 K, matching the measured 647.1 K.
3. **Measurable quantity:** critical temperature in kelvin.
   Instruments: cryostat or furnace with platinum RTD /
   thermocouple thermometry; for superconductors, the resistance
   drop at T_c (Hg: 4.15 K; Nb: 9.25 K).
4. **Standard theory:** thermodynamics of phase transitions;
   van der Waals critical point; Landau theory of second-order
   transitions; BCS theory for superconducting T_c.
5. **Prediction differing from standard theory:** none.
