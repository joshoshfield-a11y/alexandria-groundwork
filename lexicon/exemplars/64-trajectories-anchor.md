## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** phase space (x, p) of a classical system; a
   trajectory is the solution curve x(t), p(t) under the
   equations of motion.
2. **Computable form:** x(t) from Newton's second law
   m·d²x/dt² = F(x, t). Worked example: projectile launched at
   v₀ = 20 m/s, θ = 45°, g = 9.81 m/s² → range
   R = v₀² sin(2θ)/g ≈ 40.8 m; apex t = v₀ sin θ/g ≈ 1.44 s.
3. **Measurable quantity:** position in meters, velocity in m/s,
   acceleration in m/s² — sampled along the path. Instruments:
   motion-capture cameras, radar/LiDAR tracking, GPS for large
   scales; stopwatch and rangefinder for the worked example.
4. **Standard theory:** classical mechanics — Newton's laws,
   the Lagrangian/Hamiltonian formulation (trajectories as
   extremals of the action, δS = 0); the quantum analogue is
   the path integral, a separate theory not conflated here.
5. **Prediction differing from standard theory:** none.
