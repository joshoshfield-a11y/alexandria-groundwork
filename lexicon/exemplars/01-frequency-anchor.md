## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** real-valued time series x(t) of a physical
   observable, sampled at rate f_s (samples x_n = x(n/f_s)).
2. **Computable form:** f = 1/T, cycles per unit time; estimated as
   f̂ = argmax_f |X(f)| where X is the discrete Fourier transform
   of the sampled series. Angular form ω = 2πf.
3. **Measurable quantity:** hertz (s⁻¹). Instrument: frequency
   counter, or FFT peak of a digitized signal. Relative
   frequencies p_i are dimensionless; the rate itself carries s⁻¹.
4. **Standard theory:** classical wave kinematics / Fourier analysis.
   f = ω/2π; period T = 1/f.
5. **Prediction differing from standard theory:** none.
6. **Delta assessment:** no delta. This operator is the standard
   concept of frequency under a mythic name.
