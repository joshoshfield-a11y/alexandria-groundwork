## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** the n-qubit Hilbert space ℂ^(2ⁿ); a gate is a
   unitary U ∈ U(2ⁿ), U†U = I.
2. **Computable form:** Hadamard H = (1/√2)[[1,1],[1,−1]];
   H|0⟩ = (|0⟩+|1⟩)/√2. Worked example: CNOT on two qubits,
   CNOT|10⟩ = |11⟩ — the control qubit flips the target.
   Universal set: {H, T, CNOT} suffices for all unitaries.
3. **Measurable quantity:** gate fidelity F (dimensionless, 0–1);
   error rate per gate. Instruments: randomized benchmarking and
   gate-set tomography on a quantum processor; state-of-the-art
   single-qubit F ≈ 0.9999.
4. **Standard theory:** quantum information theory — unitary
   evolution, the circuit model, DiVincenzo's criteria; gates are
   generated by timed Hamiltonian pulses.
5. **Prediction differing from standard theory:** none.
