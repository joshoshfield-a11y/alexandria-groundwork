## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** a damped harmonic oscillator driven by a periodic force — a mass on a spring with damping, an RLC circuit, a wine glass under a loudspeaker.
2. **Computable form:** m·ẍ + c·ẋ + k·x = F_0·cos(ωt). The steady-state amplitude is A(ω) = F_0 / √((k − mω²)² + (cω)²), which peaks at the natural frequency ω_0 = √(k/m) (lightly damped). At resonance the amplitude is A_max ≈ F_0/(c·ω_0) and the quality factor Q = √(mk)/c measures the sharpness: the resonance bandwidth is Δω ≈ ω_0/Q. Worked example: m = 1 kg, k = 100 N/m, c = 2 N·s/m, F_0 = 10 N. Then ω_0 = 10 rad/s, Q = √(100)/2 = 5, and A_max = 10/(2·10) = 0.5 m — five times the static deflection F_0/k = 0.1 m. Drive it at 5 rad/s instead and the amplitude collapses to ≈ 0.115 m.
3. **Measurable quantity:** resonant frequency in Hz (rad/s angular); amplitude in meters (or volts for circuits); Q factor, dimensionless. Instrument: frequency sweep with a shaker table and accelerometer (mechanical) or a signal generator and oscilloscope (electrical); the amplitude-vs-frequency peak is the direct observation.
4. **Standard theory:** classical mechanics of driven oscillations; the identical mathematics governs series/parallel RLC resonance in circuit theory.
5. **Delta assessment:** none. "Resonance" names the standard driven-oscillator resonance under a proposed mythic name. The name is proposed because the slot is unnamed in every source; the physics it anchors to is textbook.

Why this name: the Choral pillar is the wave/oscillation pillar (envelopes, waveforms, wave vectors, feedback, opposition), and resonance — the pillar's central phenomenon of sympathetic response at a natural frequency — is conspicuously absent from its eighteen named operators. It completes the set rather than duplicating it.
