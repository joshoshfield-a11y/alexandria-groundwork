## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** Hilbert space H; finite-dimensional ℂ^d for the
   exemplar (d=2 worked below, generalizes).
2. **Computable form:** projector P with P² = P = P†. Action on a
   density matrix (Lüders rule): ρ → PρP / Tr(PρP).
   Worked d=2 example: P = |0⟩⟨0|, ρ = |+⟩⟨+| with |+⟩=(|0⟩+|1⟩)/√2
   → PρP/Tr(PρP) = |0⟩⟨0|, outcome probability Tr(Pρ) = 1/2.
3. **Measurable quantity:** outcome probabilities p_i = Tr(P_i ρ),
   dimensionless, measured as relative frequencies over N repeated
   trials (binomial statistics, σ = √(p(1−p)/N)).
4. **Standard theory:** projective measurement / Lüders rule; Born
   rule p_i = Tr(P_i ρ); decoherence theory for the appearance of
   collapse.
5. **The delta question:** as written, this operator IS standard
   projective measurement. Two honest options:
   - (a) **ANCHOR fallback:** admit relabel of the standard concept.
     No novelty. Documented and acceptable.
   - (b) **DEFINE with genuine novelty:** collapse probabilities
     modulated by the measuring subsystem's integration — i.e.,
     p_i = Tr(P_i ρ) · w(ι_t(measurer)) / normalization, where
     w is monotone in the consciousing measure ι_t. This predicts
     **deviations from the Born rule correlated with the measurer's
     integration** — a real, testable-in-principle departure from
     standard theory. Falsifier: repeated trials across measurers
     of differing ι_t showing no correlation.
6. **Status:** delta (b) PROPOSED, UNTESTED — a hypothesis, not a
   claim. The ANCHOR fallback (a) is recorded alongside so the
   operator is never less than honest.
