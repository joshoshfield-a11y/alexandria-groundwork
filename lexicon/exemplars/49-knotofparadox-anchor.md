## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** composite systems H_A ⊗ H_B — the tensor
   product of subsystem Hilbert spaces, dimension d_A·d_B.
2. **Computable form:** a state |ψ⟩ ∈ H_A ⊗ H_B is separable iff
   |ψ⟩ = |a⟩⊗|b⟩; otherwise entangled. Schmidt decomposition
   |ψ⟩ = Σ_k √λ_k |a_k⟩⊗|b_k⟩; Schmidt rank > 1 ⇔ entangled.
   Worked example: the Bell state
   |Φ⁺⟩ = (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2 has Schmidt coefficients
   (1/√2, 1/√2), rank 2 — maximally entangled; no product form
   exists. Tracing out B gives the reduced state ρ_A = I/2,
   maximally mixed with entropy 1 bit — the subsystem alone
   carries no information; it all lives in the correlation.
3. **Measurable quantity:** entanglement entropy S = −Σλ_k log₂λ_k
   in ebits (dimensionless); for |Φ⁺⟩, S = 1 ebit. Instrument:
   quantum state tomography, or Bell-inequality statistics from
   coincidence counts on spacelike-separated detectors.
4. **Standard theory:** quantum mechanics of composite systems;
   tensor-product structure is what makes entanglement possible;
   Bell's theorem and the observed CHSH violations (S ≈ 2√2).
5. **Delta assessment:** none. This operator is the tensor
   product — the mathematical structure binding subsystems into
   entangled wholes — under a mythic name.
