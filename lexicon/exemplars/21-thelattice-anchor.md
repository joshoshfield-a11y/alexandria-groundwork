## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** a smooth manifold M with local coordinates x^μ;
   at each point, the tangent space with inner product defined by
   the metric.
2. **Computable form:** the metric tensor g_{μν}(x), symmetric and
   invertible, giving the line element ds² = g_{μν} dx^μ dx^ν.
   Worked example: the Euclidean plane in polar coordinates,
   ds² = dr² + r²dθ², i.e. g = diag(1, r²); the circle r = R has
   circumference ∫₀^{2π} R dθ = 2πR. The inverse metric g^{μν}
   raises indices, and √|det g| supplies the invariant volume
   element dV = √|g| dⁿx — the metric measures both lengths and
   volumes.
3. **Measurable quantity:** distances and angles derived from the
   metric, in meters and radians. Instrument: rulers, laser
   interferometry, and (for spacetime) clock comparisons that read
   out the metric's geodesics.
4. **Standard theory:** Riemannian (and pseudo-Riemannian)
   geometry; the metric tensor is the foundational object of
   general relativity (Einstein field equations relate it to
   stress-energy).
5. **Delta assessment:** none. This operator is the metric tensor —
   the object that turns coordinate differences into measurable
   lengths — under a mythic name.
