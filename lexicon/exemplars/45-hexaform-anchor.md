## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** points of a periodic discrete set in the plane
   (or space): L = { n·a₁ + m·a₂ : n, m ∈ ℤ } for basis vectors
   a₁, a₂.
2. **Computable form:** the hexagonal (triangular) Bravais lattice,
   a₁ = a(1, 0), a₂ = a(1/2, √3/2). Each site has 6 nearest
   neighbors at distance a; the Wigner–Seitz cell is a hexagon.
   Worked example: graphene's lattice with a = 2.46 Å; nearest
   carbon–carbon distance a/√3 ≈ 1.42 Å, matching measurement.
3. **Measurable quantity:** the lattice constant a in meters
   (angstroms in practice). Instrument: X-ray / electron
   diffraction — Bragg peaks at reciprocal-lattice vectors
   G with |G| = 4π/(√3·a) for the hexagonal case.
4. **Standard theory:** crystallography — the 14 Bravais lattices
   in 3D (5 in 2D), of which the hexagonal lattice is one;
   and reciprocal space. Bragg's law nλ = 2d·sinθ applied to
   the hexagonal reciprocal lattice gives the diffraction peak
   positions — the lattice constant read directly off the
   pattern, closing the measurement loop.
5. **Delta assessment:** none. This operator is a lattice
   structure — the hexagonal Bravais lattice and its measurable
   constant — under a mythic name.
