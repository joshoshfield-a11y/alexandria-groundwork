## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** two periodic signals of the same frequency — two sound waves, two AC voltages, two arms of an interferometer.
2. **Computable form:** phase opposition (antiphase): x_1(t) = A·sin(ωt), x_2(t) = A·sin(ωt + π). The phase difference Δφ = π (180°); superposition gives x_1 + x_2 = 0 for equal amplitudes — complete destructive interference. More generally, x_1 + x_2 = 2A·cos(Δφ/2)·sin(ωt + Δφ/2), so the summed amplitude is 2A|cos(Δφ/2)|: maximal at Δφ = 0, zero exactly at opposition Δφ = π. Algebraic face: opposition is multiplication by −1, the order-2 element of the phase group.
3. **Measurable quantity:** phase difference in radians or degrees; null depth of the summed signal in dB relative to a single source. Instrument: dual-channel oscilloscope measuring zero-crossing offset; interferometer or noise-cancelling test rig measuring the cancellation depth.
4. **Standard theory:** wave superposition and interference; antiphase cancellation is the operating principle of active noise control.
5. **Delta assessment:** none. This operator is standard phase opposition / destructive interference under a mythic name. It is not a metaphor for conflict — the physics is the π phase shift.
