## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** electromagnetic field configurations (E, B) over
   spacetime, or equivalently the four-potential A_μ and field
   tensor F_{μν} = ∂_μA_ν − ∂_νA_μ.
2. **Computable form:** Maxwell's equations in covariant form,
   ∂_μF^{μν} = μ₀J^ν, ∂_μF̃^{μν} = 0 — one field tensor unifying
   what were separate electric and magnetic theories. Worked
   example: a plane wave E = E₀cos(kz−ωt)x̂, B = (E₀/c)cos(kz−ωt)ŷ
   with ω = ck, satisfying both wave equations from the single
   tensor.
3. **Measurable quantity:** field strengths, E in volts per meter,
   B in teslas. Instrument: field probes, Hall sensors,
   antennas measuring the unified wave.
4. **Standard theory:** classical electrodynamics (Maxwell, 1865) —
   the first and still canonical realized unification: electricity
   and magnetism are one field. (The electroweak SU(2)×U(1)
   unification extends the pattern; grand unification of all
   forces remains unobserved and is NOT claimed here.)
5. **Delta assessment:** none. The realized content of this
   operator is field unification as exemplified by Maxwell; the
   grand-unification ambition the name hints at has no measurement
   behind it and is excluded from the claim.
