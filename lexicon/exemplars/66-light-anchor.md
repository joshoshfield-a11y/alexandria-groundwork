## Hardened spec

2. **Computable form:** c = 299,792,458 m/s exactly (defined, not measured, since 1983/2019 SI). Travel time t = d/c. The "constant" reading is the anchor: c is the invariant speed, the conversion factor between space and time units.
3. **Measurable quantity:** meters per second (m/s) for propagation speed — by definition fixed; distances are what get measured against it. Instrument: time-of-flight ranging (lunar laser ranging), interferometry.
4. **Standard theory:** special relativity (Einstein 1905) — invariance of c; the SI Brochure defining the meter from c.
5. **Delta assessment:** none. This operator is the speed-of-light constant under a mythic name.
