## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** an excitable or reacting system with a state variable that can run away once pushed past a threshold — a fuel–air mixture's temperature, a neuron's membrane potential, a laser's population inversion.
2. **Computable form:** ignition is threshold crossing with positive feedback: the system ignites when the state exceeds a critical value at which self-amplification outruns losses. Thermal form (Semenov): heat release Q·A·exp(−E/RT) versus heat loss h(T − T_0); ignition occurs at the tangency where release first exceeds loss, defining the ignition temperature T_ign. Excitable-system form: ignition when the stimulus pushes the state variable past the unstable fixed point (e.g., membrane potential past threshold in Hodgkin–Huxley-type dynamics), after which the full action potential / reaction fires autonomously.
3. **Measurable quantity:** ignition temperature in °C or K (thermocouple, pyrometer); threshold voltage in volts (patch clamp, oscilloscope); ignition delay in seconds (high-speed diagnostics). All are ordinary laboratory quantities.
4. **Standard theory:** thermal explosion theory (Semenov 1928; Frank-Kamenetskii) for combustion ignition; threshold dynamics of excitable media for the generalized form.
5. **Delta assessment:** none. This operator is the standard concept of ignition — threshold crossing into self-sustaining reaction — under a mythic name. It is not a metaphor for "beginning" in general; the physics is the threshold.
