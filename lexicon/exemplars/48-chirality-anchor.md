## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** the configuration space of a molecule (or
   particle helicity states); chiral objects form non-superposable
   mirror-image pairs, R and S enantiomers.
2. **Computable form:** specific rotation [α]^T_λ = α/(l·c), where
   α is observed rotation, l path length (dm), c concentration
   (g/mL). Worked example: (S)-lactic acid has
   [α]^20_D ≈ +2.6°·mL/(dm·g); the R enantiomer gives −2.6°.
3. **Measurable quantity:** optical rotation in degrees; specific
   rotation in °·mL/(dm·g). Instrument: polarimeter. Helicity
   (particles) is measured via angular distributions in decay
   experiments.
4. **Standard theory:** stereochemistry (Cahn–Ingold–Prelog
   R/S assignment); the weak interaction violates parity, so
   chirality is also a particle-physics concept (left-handed
   fermions). Enantiomeric excess ee = (R−S)/(R+S) quantifies
   mixtures.
5. **Prediction differing from standard theory:** none.
