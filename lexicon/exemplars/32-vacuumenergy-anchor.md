## Hardened spec

1. **State space:** the quantum-field vacuum |0⟩, with
   zero-point modes ⟨0|H|0⟩ = ½Σℏω and stress-energy expectation
   ⟨T_μν⟩ = −ρ_vac g_μν.
2. **Computable form:** Casimir force between parallel plates,
   F = −π²ℏcA/(240d⁴). Worked example: A = 1 cm², d = 1 μm →
   F ≈ 1.3×10⁻⁷ N — measured to ~1% agreement
   (Mohideen & Roy 1998).
3. **Measurable quantity:** vacuum energy density ρ_vac in J/m³.
   Laboratory: Casimir force in newtons (atomic-force microscopy).
   Cosmological: the Λ-implied value ρ_vac ≈ 5.6×10⁻¹⁰ J/m³ from
   supernova data — while naive QFT mode-counting overshoots by
   ~10¹²⁰, the cosmological-constant problem.
4. **Standard theory:** quantum field theory (zero-point energy)
   plus general relativity (ΛCDM). The 120-order mismatch is a
   recognized open problem of standard theory, not a gap this
   operator fills.
5. **Prediction differing from standard theory:** none — the
   operator is not permitted to propose a solution to the
   cosmological-constant problem, and none is proposed.
