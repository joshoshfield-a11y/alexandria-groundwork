# Location Baseline Awareness — Controlled Study Design

Source protocol: UT-ITD §VI. Three steps: (1) Lexical Nullification — suspend prior cognitive mappings, attend to a minimally conceptualized state; (2) Field Entanglement — extend awareness to the sensory horizon, reframe tactile input as interference patterns; (3) Anchor Calibration — box breathing while attending to raw tactile sensation.

Credit where due: unlike most of the document, §VI names **quantitative metrics with thresholds**: MAAS increase >10% (subjective), HRV increase >5% (objective, parasympathetic shift). That is a preregistrable claim. This file turns the procedure into a study that could actually test it.

## Claim under test (narrowed)

"20 minutes of the Location Baseline Awareness protocol, practiced daily for 14 days, increases trait mindfulness (MAAS) by >10% and resting HRV (RMSSD) by >5% versus an active control."

Note what is NOT under test: "non-symbolic direct interfacing", "entanglement", wavefunction anything. Those are interpretations. The measurable claim is the attention/physiology one — the review's Level 1 (phenomenological) and Level 2 (cognitive/physiological). Level 3 (novel physical mechanism) is out of scope until Levels 1–2 replicate.

## Design

- **Arms (n=30 each, randomized):**
  - A: Full protocol (3 steps as written, audio-guided, 20 min/day).
  - B: Active control — same box-breathing + body-scan instructions, *without* the lexical-nullification / interference-pattern framing. This isolates whether the framework's specific instructions add anything over generic breath + interoception.
  - C: Waitlist (no intervention, same measurement schedule).
- **Duration:** 14 days practice + pre/post measurement, 30-day follow-up for MAAS only.
- **Primary outcomes (preregistered):** MAAS total score change (pre→post); resting 5-min RMSSD change (pre→post), measured same time of day, seated, no caffeine 2h prior.
- **Success criteria (from the source document):** mean MAAS gain >10% in A *and* significantly greater than B (p<.05, two-sided); mean RMSSD gain >5% in A *and* greater than B. Both must hold — beating waitlist alone is a breathing effect, not a protocol effect.
- **Secondary:** self-reported "raw sensation vividness" 0–10 daily (expectation check), adverse events (dissociation, anxiety spikes — stop rule: 2+ related AEs pauses the arm).
- **Blinding:** participants blind to the hypothesis (told "two relaxation techniques compared"); HRV analysis blinded to arm; MAAS is self-report (noted as a limitation).
- **Analysis plan:** ANCOVA on post scores with pre scores as covariate; intention-to-treat; preregistered before first enrollment (see PREREGISTRATION.md).

## Interpretation ladder (preregistered)

- A > B on both metrics → the protocol's specific instructions add value over breath+body-scan. Worth a replication with physiological blinding.
- A ≈ B > C → the effect is generic attentional/breathing regulation. The protocol works as a relaxation technique; the framework-specific framing is inert.
- A ≈ B ≈ C → no effect beyond measurement noise/expectation. The §VI metrics claim fails.
- Any outcome → the metaphysical interpretations (non-symbolic interfacing, field entanglement as physics) remain untested regardless. Do not let a MAAS gain launder a quantum claim.
