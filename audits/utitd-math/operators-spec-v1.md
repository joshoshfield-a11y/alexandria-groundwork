Date: 2026-10-10. Source: UT-ITD §II table ("Mathematical Mapping" and "Physical / System Function" columns as written). Method: grade what is on the page; no mechanisms invented. Consistent with RECONSTRUCTION.md (the §1.1 "Master Equation" states no equation; most mappings are labels, not derivations).

Grade key: REPRODUCES-ESTABLISHED = relabel of a known concept, no new content; SCHEMATIC = named but undefined (no variables, no computable form); ANALOGY = borrows a technical term for a claim with no demonstrated bridge; UNTESTED = shaped like a testable proposition but no data (unoccupied in this pass — nothing as written rises to a testable proposition). DEFINED is reserved for operators with a real derivation; unoccupied.
